MODULE 01 · CORE LOGIC

Direction is a hard rule.

The engine first evaluates the confirmed swing structure. With filters enabled, the higher timeframe and EMA environment must agree before a trend setup can qualify.

↑
BULLISH

Confirmed HH + HL, price above EMA 50, EMA 50 above EMA 200, and bullish higher-timeframe conditions when enabled.

↓
BEARISH

Confirmed LH + LL, price below EMA 50, EMA 50 below EMA 200, and bearish higher-timeframe conditions when enabled.
